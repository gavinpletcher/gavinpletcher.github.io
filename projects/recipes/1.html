soft choclate chip cookies

Why is this the best cookie recipe you might be asking yourself. The answer to that question can be answered by my grandma or her grandma actually. Yes, you read that right this recipe has been passes down though generations and generations, this was the bees knees during the great depression said my great grandma, and if it has stayed this long you know it has to be good.

ingredients

ingredient amount
sugar 1 cup
flour 3 cups
baking powder 1/2 teaspoon
baking soda 1 teaspoon
choclate chips 2 cups
sea salt 1 tespoon
eggs 2
brown sugar 1 cup
butter one cup
vanilla 2 teaspoons

step by step

  1. preheat oven to 375 and line 3 baking sheets with parchment paper
  2. use a medium bowl and mix flour, baking soda, baking powder , and salt
  3. cream together butter and sugars
  4. beat eggs in vanilla until light
  5. mix in the dry ingredients until fully combined
  6. add in choclate chips
  7. roll dough into balls and place them evenly on the sheets you prepared in step 1
  8. bake in oven for 8-10 minutes and take them out when theystart to turn brown
  9. let them sit until desired tempature

tips

  • take them out of the oven right before you think they are done
  • make sure you measure the flour correctly
