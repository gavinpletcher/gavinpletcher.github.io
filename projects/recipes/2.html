soft and chewey browines

for years I was craving the perfect brownie but no matter how hard I searched I couldn't find anything that came close. So I spent three months testing and eating many diffrent baches of brownies that were only fine until I found this recipe. "Whoa, I've never tasted any brownie like this before said my sister, Morgan.

ingredient amount
sugar 1 1/2 cups
flour 3/4 cup
cocoa powder 2/3 cup
powered sugar 1/2 cups
choclate chips 1/2 cup
