soft and chewey browines

for years I was craving the perfect brownie but no matter how hard I searched I couldn't find anything that came close. So I spent three months testing and eating many diffrent baches of brownies that were only fine until I found this recipe. "Whoa, I've never tasted any brownie like this before said my sister, Morgan.

ingredient amount
sugar 1 1/2 cups
flour 3/4 cup
cocoa powder 2/3 cup
powered sugar 1/2 cups
choclate chips 1/2 cup
sea salt 3/4 teaspoons
eggs 2
olive oil 1/2 cup
water 2 tablespoons
vanilla 1/2 teaspoon

instrunctions

  1. preheat oven to 325 degrees and spray a 8x8 baking dish with parchment paper with cooking spray
  2. using a medium bowl combine sugar, flour, cocoa powder, powdered sugar, choclate chips, and salt
  3. in a large bowl, whisk together eggs, olive oil, water, and vanilla
  4. sprinkle the dry mix over the wet mix and stir until mixed. repeat until fully mixed
  5. pour the batter into the pan and smooth the top using a spatula. next, bake for 40-48 minutes and let cool when finished
  6. enjoy!
